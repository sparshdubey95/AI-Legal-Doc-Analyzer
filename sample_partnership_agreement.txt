PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT

This Partnership Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into on February 20, 2024, by and between:

MICHAEL RODRIGUEZ, an individual residing at 123 Oak Street, San Francisco, CA 94102 ("Partner A"); and

JENNIFER WONG, an individual residing at 456 Pine Avenue, Palo Alto, CA 94301 ("Partner B").

ARTICLE I - FORMATION OF PARTNERSHIP

1.1 Formation. The parties hereby form a general partnership (the "Partnership") under the laws of the State of California to engage in the business of providing digital marketing consulting services.

1.2 Partnership Name. The name of the Partnership shall be "Rodriguez-Wong Digital Marketing Partners."

1.3 Principal Place of Business. The principal place of business of the Partnership shall be located at 789 Market Street, Suite 200, San Francisco, CA 94103.

1.4 Term. The Partnership shall commence on March 1, 2024, and shall continue until terminated in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement.

ARTICLE II - PURPOSE AND SCOPE

2.1 Business Purpose. The purpose of the Partnership is to provide comprehensive digital marketing services, including but not limited to:
- Social media marketing strategy and implementation
- Search engine optimization (SEO)
- Pay-per-click advertising management
- Content creation and marketing
- Email marketing campaigns
- Web analytics and reporting

2.2 Geographic Scope. The Partnership shall initially operate within the San Francisco Bay Area, with the option to expand to other markets as determined by mutual agreement of the Partners.

ARTICLE III - CAPITAL CONTRIBUTIONS

3.1 Initial Contributions. Each Partner shall make the following initial capital contributions to the Partnership:

Partner A: $75,000 in cash and office equipment valued at $25,000
Partner B: $60,000 in cash and software licenses valued at $40,000

3.2 Additional Contributions. Additional capital contributions may be made by mutual agreement of the Partners in writing.

3.3 No Interest on Contributions. No Partner shall receive interest on their capital contributions.

ARTICLE IV - PROFIT AND LOSS SHARING

4.1 Profit Sharing. Net profits of the Partnership shall be distributed as follows:
- Partner A: 55%
- Partner B: 45%

4.2 Loss Sharing. Net losses of the Partnership shall be allocated in the same proportions as profit sharing.

4.3 Timing of Distributions. Profit distributions shall be made quarterly, within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar quarter.

ARTICLE V - MANAGEMENT AND AUTHORITY

5.1 Management Rights. Both Partners shall have equal rights to participate in the management and conduct of the Partnership business.

5.2 Decision Making. Decisions requiring expenditures over $10,000 or entering into contracts with terms exceeding one year shall require unanimous consent of both Partners.

5.3 Authority Limitations. Neither Partner may, without the written consent of the other Partner:
(a) Borrow money on behalf of the Partnership in excess of $25,000
(b) Enter into any agreement that would bind the Partnership for more than two years
(c) Admit new partners to the Partnership
(d) Sell, lease, or otherwise dispose of Partnership assets exceeding $15,000 in value

ARTICLE VI - DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

6.1 Partner A Responsibilities:
- Business development and client acquisition
- Project management and client relations
- Financial management and accounting oversight

6.2 Partner B Responsibilities:
- Technical implementation of marketing campaigns
- Content creation and creative development
- Analytics, reporting, and performance optimization

6.3 Time Commitment. Each Partner agrees to devote at least 40 hours per week to Partnership business during the first two years of operations.

ARTICLE VII - BOOKS AND RECORDS

7.1 Accounting Method. The Partnership shall maintain its books and records using the accrual method of accounting.

7.2 Access to Records. Each Partner shall have full access to all Partnership books, records, and accounts during normal business hours.

7.3 Annual Accounting. The Partnership shall prepare annual financial statements to be completed within 90 days of the end of each calendar year.

ARTICLE VIII - DISSOLUTION AND TERMINATION

8.1 Events of Dissolution. The Partnership shall be dissolved upon:
(a) Mutual agreement of both Partners
(b) Death or incapacitation of either Partner
(c) Bankruptcy of the Partnership or either Partner
(d) Material breach of this Agreement by either Partner

8.2 Winding Up. Upon dissolution, the Partnership affairs shall be wound up and assets distributed in the following order:
(1) Payment of Partnership debts and liabilities
(2) Return of capital contributions to Partners
(3) Distribution of remaining assets according to profit-sharing ratios

ARTICLE IX - DISPUTE RESOLUTION

9.1 Mediation. Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall first be submitted to mediation under the Commercial Mediation Rules of the American Arbitration Association.

9.2 Arbitration. If mediation is unsuccessful, disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration conducted in San Francisco, California.

ARTICLE X - GENERAL PROVISIONS

10.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California.

10.2 Amendment. This Agreement may only be amended by written agreement signed by both Partners.

10.3 Severability. If any provision of this Agreement is deemed invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

10.4 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, or agreements.
